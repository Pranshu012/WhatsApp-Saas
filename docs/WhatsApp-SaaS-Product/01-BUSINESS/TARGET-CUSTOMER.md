# Target Customer

## Who

Indian SMBs already running customer conversations on WhatsApp, manually.

**Qualifying signals:**
- 20+ customer WhatsApp conversations per day
- Repetitive, answerable questions (timings, price, availability, location, status)
- One or two people handling messages, usually on a phone
- Already using WhatsApp Business app (many are — see D-07)
- ₹2,000/month discretionary software budget
- Someone who can attach a card to Meta without hand-holding

**Disqualifying signals:**
- Fewer than 10 conversations a day — no pain, no purchase
- Wants bulk marketing blasts — a different product, and expensive for them
- Wants deep CRM/ERP integration — not the MVP
- No card or reluctance to attach one to Meta — the model breaks
- Wants everything free
- Multiple locations and numbers — later

## Candidate verticals

**Pick one. `[DECISION REQUIRED]` D-01.**

| Vertical | Their pain | Dominant category | Meta cost/month | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dental / medical clinics** | No-shows, appointment enquiries | Utility | ₹70–150 | High willingness to pay; no-shows have a clear rupee cost |
| **Salons / spas** | Booking enquiries, reminders | Utility | ₹70–150 | High volume, low ticket |
| **Coaching / tuition centres** | Admission enquiries, fee reminders, parent updates | Utility | ₹100–250 | Seasonal peaks |
| **Boutiques / D2C** | Order status, availability, sizes | Utility + Marketing | ₹150–2,000 | Marketing pulls cost up sharply |
| **Local services** (plumbers, tutors, gyms) | Lead capture, quotes | Service + Utility | ₹70–150 | Fragmented, harder to reach |
| **B2B services** | Invoice and payment reminders | Utility | ₹50–100 | Longer sales cycle, higher retention |

**Why one vertical:** a clinic and a boutique need different templates, different automations, and
a different sales pitch. Serving both while you're still learning means doing neither well. Depth
in one vertical is also the only defensible position against funded horizontal competitors.

**The economics argument for a utility-heavy vertical:** utility is ₹0.1150 vs marketing ₹0.8631.
A clinic sending 500 reminders pays Meta ~₹68/month — trivially easy to justify. A boutique doing
marketing blasts pays ₹2,000+ — a harder conversation, and more likely to blame you for the bill.

## Buyer vs user

Usually the same person: the owner. That's good — one decision-maker, short cycle, and they feel
the pain directly.

Occasionally the owner buys and a receptionist or manager uses it. Then onboarding must include
the actual user, or adoption dies quietly.

## What they actually care about

In their order, not yours:

1. **"Will it reply to customers when I'm busy?"** — the whole reason they're talking to you
2. **"Will I lose my number or my chat history?"** — real fear; the answer is no, they own it
3. **"How much will Meta charge me?"** — answer with a specific rupee number for their volume
4. **"How long to set up?"** — under an hour, on a call with you
5. **"Can I turn it off?"** — yes, instantly

Notice what's absent: architecture, uptime, features, integrations. Nobody asks. Don't lead with
them.

## Where to find them

| Channel | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Walk in** | Highest-signal, lowest-scale. Do this for the first 10. You learn more in one visit than ten calls. |
| Referrals from customer #1 | The main engine after the first few |
| Local business WhatsApp/Facebook groups | Where they already are |
| Vertical associations (dental, salon) | Slower, but credible |
| Google Maps prospecting | Find businesses with a WhatsApp number listed |
| IndiaMART / JustDial listings | Already sales-receptive |

**Not yet:** paid ads, SEO, content marketing, cold email at scale. All need a validated pitch
first, and you don't have one until D-01 is closed.

## Objections and honest answers

| "..." | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Why two bills?" | "Because we don't mark up Meta. You pay them what they charge — usually ₹70–150 for your volume. Most tools hide a 20% markup in one bill." |
| "Will I lose my WhatsApp number?" | "No. The account is yours, in your name. You can disconnect us anytime and keep everything." |
| "I already reply myself." | "You do — and you're good at it. This handles the 20 identical questions so you can answer the 5 that need you." |
| "₹1,999 is expensive." | "How many enquiries do you miss after 8pm? What's one lost appointment worth?" |
| "Can I try free?" | "You can start and cancel anytime. I'd rather you pay and hold me to it than test something nobody's committed to." |
| "Is it safe?" | "Your data is isolated, encrypted, backed up. And it's your WhatsApp account, not ours." |

## What we are not selling to, yet

Enterprises · multi-location chains · agencies reselling to their own clients · businesses
outside India · anyone wanting cold-outreach marketing (a Meta policy risk, and it will hurt their
quality rating and eventually your app).
